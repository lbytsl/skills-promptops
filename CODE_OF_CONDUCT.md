# Code of Conduct

English · [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and open technical community. Participants must respect different experiences and viewpoints, focus discussion on verifiable technical arguments, and avoid harassment, discrimination, privacy violations, or personal attacks.

Report misconduct through the private contact method published by the maintainers. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or take other necessary action. Maintainers are held to the same standard.

This policy is a concise adaptation of the core principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
